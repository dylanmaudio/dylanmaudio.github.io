//  What it does

MIDI Bridge carries MIDI over TCP to and from your dLive, and ships with a live, decoded message monitor — every fader, mute and scene crossing the wire in plain language, with real console descriptions instead of raw MIDI bytes. Free, and the foundation the other apps build on.

  • MIDI over TCP, to and from the console
  • Live decoded monitor — real console descriptions, not raw MIDI bytes
  • Advanced monitor mode for protocol-level detail
  • At-a-glance menu-bar status indicator
  • Free — no licence, no trial timer
